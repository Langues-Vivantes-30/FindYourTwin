Classroom speaking activity

Find Your Twin

Create identity cards and help pupils find the classmate who shares their profile.

1. Set up your class

Choose the number of pupils and the information pupils will use to describe themselves.

Choose a number from 6 to 30. For an odd number, one extra identity card will be created.
Choose up to 3 criteria. You can unlock more when needed.

2. Add the vocabulary

Enter the possible answers for each selected criterion. Age requires exactly 2 values; all other criteria require at least 3. Empty fields are ignored.

Select one or more criteria above to enter the vocabulary.

3. Preview

The first pair of identity cards is shown below.

In the print window, choose “Save as PDF” as the destination. Print settings: A4, portrait, scale 100% (or default).
